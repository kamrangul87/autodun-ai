MOT cost in the UK: current maximum fees (2026)

By Kamran Gul · Updated 9 November 2026 · Fees checked against GOV.UK

Current maximum MOT fees (Great Britain), from GOV.UK — MOT costs:

  • Car (Class 4, up to 8 passenger seats): £54.85
  • Standard motorcycle (Class 1 or 2): £29.65
  • Motorcycle with sidecar: £37.80

You do not pay VAT on the fee. Test centres may charge less than the maximum, but they cannot charge more.

If you have seen older MOT cost guides (for example figures quoted for 2023), the statutory maximum for a car is still £54.85. The DVSA MOT test fees and appeals poster published on GOV.UK (updated 3 June 2026) still lists these maximums. The current poster PDF notes that the fees apply from 6 April 2010.

How much is a motorbike MOT in 2026?

The maximum motorbike MOT fee is £29.65. This applies to Class 1 bikes (up to 200cc) and Class 2 bikes (over 200cc). With a sidecar, the maximum is £37.80. A motorbike needs its first MOT at 3 years old, then one every year. The full list is in the MOT inspection manual for motorcycles.

For a deeper dive on bikes specifically — fees, checks and common fails — see our motorbike MOT cost guide.

Why garages charge less than the maximum, and how to pay less

  • Compare a few local test centres. Prices can differ between garages on the same street.
  • Ask about retests before you book. Retest rules and charges differ between centres, so check what you would pay if the car fails. See our guide to MOT retest costs and free retest rules.
  • Book early. You can get an MOT up to a month (minus a day) before it runs out and keep the same renewal date (GOV.UK).
  • Do simple checks yourself first. Bulbs, wiper blades, screenwash, tyre tread and warning lights are common, easy-to-avoid fail points.
  • Check past results. Look up your car's earlier advisories with an MOT history check and fix known issues before the test. Our guide to MOT advisories explains what each one means.
  • Get a quick risk estimate. The free Autodun MOT predictor highlights likely problem areas for your car.

Remember: you can be fined up to £1,000 for driving without a valid MOT (GOV.UK).

MOT price in Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland has different rules and processes for vehicles registered there. For official NI fees, check nidirect. For Great Britain fees, see the GOV.UK page linked above.

MOT cost FAQ

How much is an MOT in 2026?

The maximum for a car is £54.85. Many garages charge less. There is no VAT on the fee.

What is the maximum MOT fee for a car on GOV.UK?

£54.85 for a Class 4 car with up to 8 passenger seats.

How much is a motorbike MOT?

Up to £29.65 for a standard motorcycle, or £37.80 with a sidecar. Our motorbike MOT cost guide covers Class 1 and 2 fees, what is checked, and how to save.

Has the MOT fee gone up in 2026?

GOV.UK still lists £54.85 for cars and £29.65 for standard motorcycles. The DVSA MOT test fees poster published on GOV.UK (updated 3 June 2026) notes that these maximum fees apply from 6 April 2010.

Does the MOT fee include repairs?

No. The fee covers the test only. Repairs are quoted and charged separately.

When does my car need its first MOT?

By the third anniversary of its registration, then every year. Taxis and ambulances need one from 1 year old.
