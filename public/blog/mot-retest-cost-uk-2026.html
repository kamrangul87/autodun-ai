MOT Retest Costs UK 2026: When It’s Free, Partial Fees and Timing Rules

By the Autodun Team · Updated 4 November 2026 · Rules verified with GOV.UK

Short answer: an MOT retest can be free in specific situations — for example, if you leave your vehicle at the same test centre and it’s retested within 10 working days. If you take it away for repairs and return it within 10 working days, you’ll usually get a partial retest and the centre may charge a fee (DVSA allows up to half the statutory maximum). If you return by the end of the next working day and only certain minor items are checked, the partial retest can also be free.

When MOT retests are free

  • Left at the same MOT centre: If your vehicle stays at the test centre and is retested within 10 working days, you only need a partial retest — no fee is charged.
  • Next working day on specific items: If you take it away but bring it back to the same centre before the end of the next working day, and only one or more of the listed minor items are checked (e.g. lamps, mirrors, registration plates, wipers, etc.), the partial retest is free.

Source: GOV.UK — Retest after a repair.

When a partial retest fee applies

If you remove the vehicle from the MOT centre for repair and return it to the same centre within 10 working days, the station can charge a partial retest fee. DVSA guidance allows up to half the statutory maximum test fee for the vehicle class.

Source: MOT testing guide — Accounts & fees and DVSA VT9A poster.

Timing rules: 10 working days and next working day

  • 10 working days means excluding weekends and public holidays. The count starts the day after the initial test.
  • Only one partial re-examination is allowed per full test.
  • If you miss the window or the centre has changed ownership, you’ll usually need a full retest and pay the full fee.

Source: MOT testing guide — Accounts & fees.

How to avoid paying for a retest

  • Ask about retests before you book. Centres set their own policies within DVSA rules — confirm costs up front.
  • Fix easy items immediately. If only listed minor items are involved, returning by the next working day can qualify for a free partial retest.
  • Use your MOT history. Check previous advisories/fail points and sort them first. See our MOT history guide and advisories explained.
  • Get a risk estimate. The free Autodun MOT predictor highlights likely problem areas before your test.

MOT retest FAQ

Is an MOT retest free?

Yes, in some cases — see the rules above.

How much is a partial MOT retest?

Up to half the statutory maximum MOT fee for your vehicle class (charged at the centre’s discretion).

Do the same rules apply to motorbikes?

Yes — timing and ‘next working day’ free partial retest rules apply across vehicle classes. See GOV.UK for details.

What if I miss the 10 working day window?

You’ll usually need a full MOT retest and pay the full fee.
