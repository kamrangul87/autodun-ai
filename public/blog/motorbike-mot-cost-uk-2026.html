Motorbike MOT Cost UK 2026: Fees, What’s Checked and How to Save

By the Autodun Team · Updated 4 November 2026 · Fees verified against GOV.UK

Short answer: the most a test centre can charge for a motorbike MOT in 2026 is £29.65 (Classes 1 & 2). With a sidecar the maximum is £37.80. There’s no VAT on the MOT fee, and garages are allowed to charge less (GOV.UK — MOT costs).

Official motorbike MOT fees in 2026 (DVSA)

The government sets a maximum MOT fee for each vehicle class. For motorcycles, the official caps are:

  • Class 1/2 motorcycle: £29.65
  • Motorcycle with sidecar: £37.80

Source: GOV.UK – MOT costs and the DVSA VT9A poster. Figures checked 4 November 2026.

Remember: these are maximum fees — many garages charge less.

What’s checked on a motorcycle MOT

Motorcycle MOTs focus on core roadworthiness and safety. Typical inspection items include:

  • Brakes — performance and condition of pads/discs and controls
  • Tyres & wheels — tread depth, condition, size/type, valve condition
  • Lights & electrics — headlamp, indicators, tail/stop lamps, horn, wiring
  • Steering & suspension — head bearings, forks, shocks, swingarm
  • Frame & bodywork — corrosion, damage, sharp edges
  • Exhaust & emissions/noise (where applicable)
  • Throttle & clutch — operation and condition
  • Registration plate & VIN

See the official DVSA MOT inspection manual for motorcycles for full details.

Common motorbike MOT fail reasons

  • Tyres below legal tread or with damage/cuts
  • Brake pads/discs worn or uneven performance
  • Lights not working, incorrect colour, or poor headlamp aim
  • Chain & sprockets excessively worn or poorly adjusted
  • Suspension leaks or play in head bearings
  • Number plate incorrect size/format, obscured, or damaged

How to pay less (and pass first time)

  • Compare local MOT centres. Prices vary even on the same street.
  • Book early. You can test up to a month (minus a day) before expiry and keep the same renewal date (GOV.UK).
  • Do simple checks yourself first. Bulbs, indicators, tyre pressures, chain condition, horn, number plate, mirrors.
  • Check past results. Look up advisories/fails with our MOT history guide and fix known issues beforehand.
  • Get a quick risk estimate. The free Autodun MOT predictor highlights likely problem areas for your bike.

Retests and partial retest fees

If your bike fails, there are situations where the retest is free:

  • Left at the same MOT centre: retested within 10 working days → no retest fee.
  • Taken away and returned the next working day: if only specific listed minor items are checked → no retest fee.

If you take it away for repairs and return it to the same centre within 10 working days, you’ll usually get a partial retest; the centre may charge a partial retest fee (DVSA allows up to half the statutory maximum). In all other cases, you may need a full retest and full fee.

Sources: GOV.UK — Retest after a repair and MOT testing guide — Accounts & fees.

Motorbike MOT cost FAQ

How much is a motorbike MOT?

Up to £29.65 for standard motorcycles; £37.80 with a sidecar. Garages can charge less.

Does the MOT fee include repairs?

No. The fee covers the test only. Repairs are quoted and charged separately by the garage.

When does a bike need its first MOT?

At 3 years from first registration, then every year. Check your due date with the DVSA service.

Where can I check my bike’s MOT history?
