Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Autodun operates autodun.com and related tools including ev.autodun.com, mot.autodun.com and ai.autodun.com. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, which third parties receive it, and how to contact us about your data.

The data controller is Kamran Gul, operator of autodun.com.

What we collect and why

Contact form submissions

When you use the contact form at autodun.com/contact, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message. This data is submitted to Formspree (formspree.io), our third-party form processor, solely to allow us to respond to your enquiry. Contact form messages are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (responding to your enquiry).

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID: G-ZPK0SR60XR) to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics loads only if you accept analytics cookies; you can change your choice at any time via Cookie settings in the footer. It collects page views, approximate geographic location, device and browser type, and session duration. Google Analytics uses cookie identifiers, which are pseudonymous personal data. Google acts as our data processor under its standard terms.

Lawful basis: consent. If you reject or never accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics is never loaded.

Vehicle Registration Numbers (VRNs)

The MOT Predictor tool (hosted at mot.autodun.com) processes VRNs you enter. Registration numbers may be personal data. We send them to DVSA only to retrieve MOT history and do not store them.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (providing the tool you requested).

Hosting and server logs

Our hosting provider, Vercel, receives your IP address and standard request information (URL, time, browser user agent) whenever you load a page or use a tool, and keeps short-lived request logs for security and operation of the service.

Lawful basis: legitimate interests (securely delivering the website).

Cookies

  • cookie_consent — Stores your analytics cookie choice (accepted or rejected) for autodun.com and its subdomains. 6 months. Strictly necessary.
  • sidebar:state — Remembers whether the navigation sidebar is open or closed. 7 days. Strictly necessary (UI state, no personal data).
  • _ga, _ga_ZPK0SR60XR — Google Analytics identifiers — set only if you accept analytics cookies. Up to 2 months. Analytics (consent).

You can change your analytics choice at any time via Cookie settings.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict how we process your data;
  • data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time (for analytics, via Cookie settings in the footer) — this does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew it;
  • lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk).

To exercise any of these rights, email info@autodun.com.
